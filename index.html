Ready-made MediaPipe Pose model (no training). Draws shoulders, elbows, wrists and hips over the live camera view. The model is downloaded the first time it is switched on (internet needed once). The rule-based draw/release detector runs while it is on (yellow FULL DRAW, green RELEASE). The log records pose points plus the microphone/BT signal as the true release time, to tune the detector. Playback zoom

Two fingers: pinch to zoom, drag to move. PC: mouse wheel zooms at the cursor; Shift + drag (or right button) moves the picture. The zoom stays for the next clips until you press Reset zoom (top left in playback). SAVE writes the clip with this zoom and offset (and the grid, if shown).
